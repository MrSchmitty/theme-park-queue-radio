# Park Radio — Voice Design prompts

Ten hosts. For each one, paste the **description** into the voice description box in
ElevenLabs Voice Design, and the **preview line** into the text box so you hear the
voice saying something it will actually say on air.

Generate two or three per description — Voice Design varies between runs, and the
third result is often better than the first. Save the ones you like, then assign
them per host in Park Radio's Booth panel.

---

## Mansion host

**Description**

```
An elderly British gentleman in his seventies with a deep, resonant voice that carries a hollow, cavernous quality. Aristocratic Received Pronunciation, impeccably polite. Speaks very slowly with long deliberate pauses, a faint smile audible beneath every word. Dry funereal warmth, as though delivering grim news he finds mildly amusing. Clean studio recording with a hint of large-room air.
```

**Preview line**

```
Do come in. The gallery is rather full this evening, but I'm certain we can find room for one more.
```

## Tuning notes

- **Too flat or robotic?** Add a specific physical detail — a rasp, a wheeze, a
  catch in the throat. Concrete beats adjectives every time.
- **Right character, wrong age?** Change the stated age by ten years and regenerate.
- **Accent drifting?** Name a region rather than a country.
- **Recording feels sterile?** The room description at the end of each prompt is
  doing real work. Strengthen it if the voice sounds booth-bound.

Once your voices are saved, `make-voices.py` generates all fifty clips in one run.
