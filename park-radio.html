Park Radio doesn't play the audio itself here — it directs the Spotify app on your phone, which is what makes lock-screen playback work. Keep Spotify installed and signed in, and open it once so it appears in the device list.
Setup, once: create an app at developer.spotify.com/dashboard, paste the redirect URI above into its settings, copy the Client ID here, then Connect. Your account must be added to the app's user allowlist under Settings → User Management. Requires Premium.
